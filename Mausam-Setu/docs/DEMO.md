# One-minute Judge Mode walkthrough

0–10 seconds: Open the homepage. Select Judge Mode. Point out Demo data and the fixed scenario date; it cannot be confused with actual observations.

10–25 seconds: Choose Extreme heat and Fitness + Health. The outdoor card merges both interests, includes exposure guidance and avoids inventing a workout window if limits fail.

25–35 seconds: Choose Destination rain and Travel. Show the umbrella/rain-protection recommendation derived from forecast probability.

35–45 seconds: Choose Agricultural rainfall and Agriculture. Show 18 mm over 24 complete forecast intervals and the instruction to check soil before irrigation decisions.

45–55 seconds: Open Why this for me? Show input reasons and bounded score components. The number is rank, not a probability of safety.

55–60 seconds: Choose No available data. Show that the application removes recommendations rather than fabricating conditions.

Closing line: **Same weather. Different users. Personalized actions.**

Optional: Pleasant day demonstrates a screened daylight activity window. Poor AQI and Foggy morning exercise different advisories. Reset restores the heat scenario, Lucknow and Fitness + Health.
